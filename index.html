      Help & support
      How do I start a chat?

      Search a person by full name or @username and send a chat request. You can chat once they accept.

      How do locked chats and the vault work?

      A passcode protects locked chats and the vault on this device. Vault files are encrypted before upload. Your original photo stays in your gallery, so delete it there yourself once it is in the vault.

      Is my phone number visible?

      No. Phone numbers are never shown to other members.
